# Secret Shopper — Demo Script

> **Target**: Casual investor intro. Goal is to gauge interest and land a follow-up meeting.
> **Time**: 2-3 minutes for the full walkthrough.

---

## The 60-Second Hook

> "Every time you buy something online, you hand over your real email, your real card, and then you get buried in marketing spam forever. You lose track of returns, miss price drops, and never use the promo codes sitting in your inbox. Secret Shopper puts an AI agent between you and every retailer. It gives you a fake email, a capped wallet, and an agent that watches prices, catches promos, and tells you the perfect time to buy."

## The 3 Questions They'll Ask

### "How do you make money?"

> "Subscription model — $5-10 per month for premium features like AI predictions, unlimited aliases, and advanced retailer analytics. There's also potential affiliate revenue on deals we surface. The wallet float is NOT the business model — we're not trying to be a bank. We're selling the intelligence layer."

### "What exists today?"

> "Our collaborator has two working products: a Chrome extension for email anonymization that's already in market, and a gift card/virtual card product. Secret Shopper is the unified vision that ties those together with AI behavioral prediction and a mobile-first dashboard. The extension is the bridge — you can see it right here in the status bar. This isn't starting from zero."

### "What's the moat?"

> "The closed loop. Honey does coupons. Privacy.com does cards. Shop app does tracking. Capital One Shopping does price alerts. Nobody — nobody — connects alias to scrape to predict to checkout in one flow.
>
> And here's the kicker: the behavioral prediction data gets more defensible over time. We're building a dataset of which retailers send promos, when they send them, for what cart values, after how many days. That's a proprietary intelligence layer that compounds with every user, every cart, every purchase."

## Demo Tips

- **Don't rush the Cold Cases section.** That's the "aha" moment — strategic abandonment is the wedge.
- **Let them tap around.** If they want to explore, let them. The mockup is built for it.
- **If they ask about the extension**: It already exists. Point to the "Extension connected" badge on the dashboard.
- **If they ask about regulation**: The wallet is pre-funded, not stored-value. We're not holding deposits — users load specific amounts for specific retailers. This is closer to gift cards than banking.
- **If they ask about acquisition**: The extension is the top of funnel. Free email aliasing gets them in the door. The AI predictions and wallet are the premium upgrade.
