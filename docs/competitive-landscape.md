## The Insight

Every competitor does 1-2 of these things well. Secret Shopper's thesis is that **the value compounds when they're connected.**

- **Honey** finds you a code at checkout — but doesn't know your cart was abandoned on purpose, doesn't track if the code was the best one coming, and doesn't protect your email or card.
- **Privacy.com** gives you virtual cards — but has no shopping intelligence. It's a payment tool, not a shopping agent.
- **Shop App** tracks your packages — but doesn't help you buy smarter, doesn't block spam, and doesn't predict deals.
- **Capital One Shopping** alerts you to price drops — but only works for Capital One customers, doesn't offer aliases, and has no behavioral prediction.

**Secret Shopper connects the full loop**: alias email → promo scraping → cart monitoring → AI prediction → capped wallet checkout. No one else does this.

## Competitor Deep Dive

### Honey (PayPal)
- **What they do**: Browser extension that auto-applies coupon codes at checkout. Droplist for price tracking.
- **Strengths**: Massive install base (17M+ users). Acquired by PayPal for $4B.
- **Gaps**: Reactive, not predictive. No email protection. No payment isolation. No cart intelligence. Post-acquisition innovation has stalled.

### Privacy.com
- **What they do**: Virtual debit cards with per-merchant spending limits.
- **Strengths**: Strong privacy positioning. Merchant-locked cards. Good UX.
- **Gaps**: Pure payment tool. No shopping intelligence, no deal finding, no tracking, no email aliasing. Users still need to find deals themselves.

### Shop App (Shopify)
- **What they do**: Package tracking aggregator. Shop Pay checkout.
- **Strengths**: Deep Shopify ecosystem integration. Large user base from order confirmations.
- **Gaps**: Only tracks Shopify merchants well. No price intelligence. No email protection. No abandoned cart strategy. Primarily a post-purchase tool.

### Capital One Shopping (formerly Wikibuy)
- **What they do**: Price comparison, coupon codes, price drop alerts. Credit redemption.
- **Strengths**: Price history data. Backed by Capital One's resources.
- **Gaps**: Locked to Capital One ecosystem for best rewards. No email aliasing. No payment isolation. No behavioral prediction. No cart strategy.

## Why the Closed Loop Matters

```
Traditional flow:
  Browse → Buy → Get spammed → Miss deals → Overpay → Lose track of returns

Secret Shopper flow:
  Browse → Alias shields your identity
       → Agent monitors your cart
       → AI predicts the best time to buy
       → Capped wallet protects your spending
       → All tracking, returns, and promos in one place
```

The defensible moat isn't any single feature — it's the **behavioral prediction dataset** that compounds over time. Every abandoned cart, every promo code timing, every retailer pattern builds a proprietary intelligence layer that gets more accurate with scale.
